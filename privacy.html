Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

OAuth Info describes a private, single-user automation. This informational website does not request that visitors sign in or submit personal data.

Google account access

When authorized by the account owner, the automation uses Google OAuth to read and update the owner's spreadsheets and, where necessary, access associated Google Drive files. Google permissions are requested during the authorization process.

Use of data

Data accessed through Google services is used to perform operations requested by the account owner. It is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website.

Storage and control

The automation is privately hosted. Authentication credentials are stored in the automation environment, and spreadsheet data remains in the account owner's chosen services. The owner can revoke access using Google Account security settings and can delete their own stored records.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through GitHub issues. Do not post private information in public issues.
